# Sri Ganesh Pooja Vidhanam Android Application Privacy Policy

**App:** Sri Ganesh Pooja Vidhanam\
**Developer:** SLA Technologies\
**Last updated:** 10 November 2026

## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how the **Sri Ganesh Pooja Vidhanam** mobile application ("App") handles information. The App
provides devotional content and features relating to Lord Ganesha, including Virtual Pooja and Vinayaka Chavithi Vratham.

Based on the App's current functionality, the App does not collect or transmit users' personal information.

## 2. Information We Collect

The App does not require users to create an account and does not collect, store on our servers, or transmit personal information such as
names, email addresses, phone numbers, postal addresses, contacts, precise location, photos, videos, personal files, or payment information.

## 3. Analytics and Advertising

The App does not use analytics services or advertising networks and does not display third-party advertisements.

## 4. How We Use and Share Information

Because the App does not collect or transmit personal information, we do not use, sell, rent, or share users' personal information with third parties.

## 5. Data Storage and Security

The App does not maintain a server-side database of users' personal information. Information that may be handled independently by your
device or operating system is subject to the applicable device and operating-system settings.

## 6. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users. If you believe personal information has been provided to us in connection with the App, please contact us using the details in the Contact section.

## 7. Third-Party Websites or Services

If a future version of the App includes links to third-party websites or services, those services will be governed by their own privacy policies. 

We encourage users to review those policies before using the relevant services.

## 8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the App's features or applicable requirements change. Any revised policy will be published at the same location with an updated revision date.
